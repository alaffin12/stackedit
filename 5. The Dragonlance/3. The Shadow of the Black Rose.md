# Dargaard Keep

> You step into Raistlin's portal, and out into the ruins of a village.  This place, shrouded in fog and mist, bears no small resemblance to the Mournlands.  And yet it is different.  A different kind of magic perverts this land.  As dark and as twisted as the tale of the Fall of Cyre, but different.  
>
> Ahead of you a series of jagged switchbacks cut into a cliff face climb towards a narrow pass in the mountains.  Crouched between two sharp peaks the massive charred black edifice of what must be Dargaard Keep looms the shadow of a forgotten age.   From this distance you can see little of the Keep itself obscured by a thick curtain wall Yet silhouetted against a dark sky you can perceive the shape of t he Keep’s central tower rising hundreds of feet straight into the air before expanding at the apex like a black rose in full bloom.

## Ground Level
### A1. The Bridge
> The brittle wood of the drawbridge creaks ominously beneath your feet.  Ahead of you black hoof prints lead the way past what was once the main gate - now another part of the ruin left only partially formed.   An endless wind howls through the bottomless chasm that separates Dargaard Keep from its main approach.  The curtain wall is guarded by five towers surrounding the Keep Precarious walk ways hanging in thin air connect the guard towers to the Keep itself.  The central tower looms overhead.  From some where high above the sound of bitter keening rises over the cold wind.

Anyone who has studied Krynn during their time in Sigil will know that Dargaard Keep was considered the epitome of defensive fortifications pre-cataclysm.  It was besieged for months in the lead up to the cataclysm, but did not fall until it was destroyed by the wrath of the gods themselves.  

A DC 10 Intelligence (Nature) check reveals the tracks belong to an unusually large horse.  A DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) check reveals the tracks belong to a Nightmare.
