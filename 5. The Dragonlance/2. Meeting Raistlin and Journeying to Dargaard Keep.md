# Journey to Krynn
> You step into the Orrey of the Spheres and the device activates, whirling brightly around you as reality begins to dissolve and reform.  You focus it on one name.  Raistlin Majere.  The name echoes through the cosmos, and towards the realm of Krynn, drawing the orrey and you with it.  Around you you see the image of the mage form.  You have met him.  In the first battle in Sigil.  With golden skin and hourglass eyes.  Then it blinks.  The image shifts.  Something darker.  An ancient, withered figure, with eyes that burn of malevolence.  You feel the fire in his eyes.  His heart.  It burns you...
>
> And then you step into a clearing in the woods, near the ruins of an ancient stone shrine.  Nearby a young man - the mage, from the image, Raistlin Majere, sits against a stone pillar, robed in red and reading a book by torch light.  
>
> "Curious..." he says, without looking up "...I requested solitude.  It seems I was misheard."  He looks up at the party, his hour glass eyes shimmering in the moon light.  "Who are you.  And why have you intruded upon my study?"
