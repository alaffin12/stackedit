Dragonmarks are physical manifestation of the ancient Draconic Prophecy.  They have appeared on seven of the common races.  They are a status symbol, and provide the bearer with magic talents.  They are ranked in increasing size, complexity and power from Least, to Lesser, to Greater and finally to Siberys.

Dragonmarks usually manifest around adolescence, usually under specific circumstances.  Dragonmarked Houses will try and place likely heirs into trials that will cause their Dragonmark to manifest, although the nature of these trials varies by mark.  Dragonmarks are not strictly genetic, but a child of two dragonmarked parents is about 50/50 to develop a mark of their own.  

An 'heir' is anyone born into a dragonmark family.  People who work for a house, both dragonmarked and not, identify themselves with a "d'" before the house name (i.e. Merrix d'Cannith).  Regional leaders are called Barons.  Most houses are led by a Matriarch or a Patriarch (although some are led by councils).  'Excoriates' are dragonmarked individuals who have been exiled from the house.  'Foundlings' are dragonmarked individuals who develop a mark outside a known bloodline.  

The Korth Edicts forbid houses from owning land, holding titles of nobility or maintaining a military force (except House Deneith).  However they were established long ago, and there is a feeling that the edicts are outdated, and that the kingdoms of the world do not possess the power to enforce them.
