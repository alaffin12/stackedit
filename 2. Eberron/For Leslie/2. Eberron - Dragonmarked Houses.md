Dragonmarks are physical manifestation of the ancient Draconic Prophecy.  They have appeared on seven of the common races.  They are a status symbol, and provide the bearer with magic talents.  They are ranked in increasing size, complexity and power from Least, to Lesser, to Greater and finally to Siberys.

Dragonmarks usually manifest around adolescence, usually under specific circumstances.  Dragonmarked Houses will try and place likely heirs into trials that will cause their Dragonmark to manifest, although the nature of these trials varies by mark.  Dragonmarks are a recessive genetic trait.
