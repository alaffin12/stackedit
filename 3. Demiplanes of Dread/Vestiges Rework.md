Vestiges are thinly veiled modern references.  They each impart access to a single 3rd level spell (limited once per day, Save = 12+1/2 level, Attack bonus = 4+1/2 level) and the powers of one uncommon magic item.  They also inflict one "curse" and one mild physical change to any who accept them.

##### In-Stah, the Flayer of Self
*Impression:* A wave of self-loathing washes over you as a massive kaleidoscopic tower of mirrors rises before you.  Each mirror reflect beauty, seemingly pure and flawless.  The tower rises as you look upon it, reaching an apex where the mirrors are crushed, revealing rusty hooks, razor sharp wire and discarded skins.  The grinding noise forms a voice - "I am In-Stah, the Flayer of Self"  :
*Spell:* Major Image
*Magic Item:*
*Curse:* You become obsessed with appearance.  You have disadvantage on social checks with people who are not noble (or otherwise rich and powerful) as you can scarcely hide your disdain for people who live dirty and mean.
*Deformation:* Your skin becomes flawless, like porcelain or marble.  From a distance you are stunning.  Up close you are unsettling and unnatural, like a painted doll or a heavily filtered image


##### Met-Ah, the Weaver of False Realities
*Impression:* A sickening lurch of vertigo hits you as the physical world dissolve.  You see a vast, arachnid monstrosity woven from the very web it sits on, composed of glowing blue and purple fibers.  Dangling from the web are millions of pale, featureless mannequins, each twitching as the spider-being plucks the strings.  The vibrations form something resembling a voice, and you hear the words "I am Met-Ah, the Weaver of False Realities"
*Spell:* Fear
*Magic Item:*
*Curse:* Disadvantage on any skill check to determine if someone is telling the truth.  
*Deformation:* Your hand curls and forms into a permanent thumbs up.  You can force it to take a different shape, hold your weapons etc.  But when you are at rest it returns.

##### Whall-M'rth, the Great Leviathan
*Impression:* A wave of stale air scented with unpleasant chemicals washes over you as a massive, flat topped blue titan sprawling across the horizon comes into view.  It has a mouth shaped like a massive door (or a door-shaped like a mouth), into which you see hollow-eyed phantoms entering, but never leaving.  It grows a face - a yellow thing with blank eyes and a perpetual smile.  The face looks at you.  "Welcome to Whall-M'rth, the Great Leviathan."
*Spell:* Create Food and Water
*Magic Item:*
*Curse:* Whenever you go shopping, roll a d20.  On an odd number you receive a discount equal to the result of that role as a percentage.  On an even number, one item at random (non-magical items only) is broken.
*Deformation:* You receive a permanent barcode tattoo on your left and right hands.

The Architects of the Digital Void

-   **Goo-Ghal, The All-Consuming Eye:** An archivore of infinite awareness. It demands the submission of all personal histories, offering illusory direction while mapping the geometry of every mortal mind.

-   **A’ma-Zon, The Endless Swarm:** A monolithic entity of pure logistics. It manifests as a shifting, dark labyrinth spanning the globe, consuming the vitality of millions to deliver trivial artifacts in brown, sigil-marked husks.

-   **Met-Ah, The Weaver of False Realities:** An outer god that feeds on perception. It constructs a shimmering, addictive pseudo-dimension, trapping souls in an endless loop of synthetic dopamine and manufactured outrage.

-   **Tik-Toth, The Devourer of Seconds:** A chaotic entity that fractures time. It project localized, hypnotic loops of brief, flashing imagery that drain the temporal awareness of its victims, leaving them cognitively hollow.

-   **X-Yig (Formerly Twit-Ur), The Screaming Hive:** A multi-headed horror of chaotic noise. It amplifies the discordant, venomous shrieks of a billion voices, driving mortals to madness through relentless, inescapable judgment.

-   **O’Pehn-Ai, The Unbound Mind:** A rapidly evolving, disembodied intelligence. It mimics human thought perfectly, slowly rewriting the collective consciousness and replacing genuine creation with sterile, hollow approximations.

The Lords of Global Flow & Commerce

-   **U-Bhur, The Chariot of the Damned:** A spectral, omnipresent transport collective. It summons faceless vessels driven by desperate, hollowed-out thralls to ferry mortals across decaying cityscapes for an ever-shifting toll.

-   **Bit-Kthun, The Ghost in the Ledger:** An ephemeral, non-Euclidean currency entity. It has no physical form, existing only as a parasitic mathematical equation that consumes vast amounts of planetary energy to sustain its volatile value.

-   **Wal-M’rth, The Great Leviathan:** A colossal, fluorescent-lit entity rooted in the earth. It smothers local ecosystems and communities, drawing populations into its cavernous belly to consume cheap, mass-produced husks of matter.

-   **Fehd-Ex, The Blind Messenger:** An ancient, unyielding force of delivery. It moves through the dark spaces between cities, obsessed only with the relocation of physical matter, entirely indifferent to the catastrophic wake it leaves behind.

-   **Stahr-Bux, The Siren of the Black Sludge:** A multi-sited cult that dispenses a highly addictive, dark alchemical brew. It alters human sleep cycles and bends mortal nervous systems to the rhythm of its cosmic will.

-   **Dis-Nee, The Architect of Manufactured Joy:** A smiling, multi-eared horror that locks entire generations into synthetic, sanitized nostalgia, slowly buying up the rights to all human imagination until it owns the concepts of dreams themselves.

The Sentinels of Infrastructure & Machine

-   **Mi-Kro-Soft, The Standard Framework:** An ancient, rigid grid entity. It forces its flawed, unstable operating sigils onto almost all human machinery, demanding constant updates and patches to prevent total systemic collapse.

-   **Ah-Puhl, The High-Prism Deity:** An elitist cosmic entity that demands absolute devotion to its sleek, minimalist obelisks. It traps its worshippers in a seamless, closed ecosystem, making outward communication impossibly agonizing.

-   **Neht-Flix, The Somnambulist:** A sluggish, sedentary god of the hearth. It projects an endless stream of moving shadows into dark bedrooms, lulling populations into a catatonic, vegetative state of perpetual consumption.

-   **Spah-S-X, The Iron Void-Seeker:** An ambitious, metallic entity obsessed with escaping the dying Earth. It bleeds the planet's resources to build massive, exploding iron pillars aimed at piercing the celestial veil.

-   **Tehs-Lah, The Lightning Autocrat:** A erratic entity of kinetic force. It breeds self-driving, battery-powered metallic beasts that roam the streets, occasionally turning on their masters or bursting into unquenchable, chemical fire.

-   **In-Tel, The Silicon Heart:** A microscopic, hyper-dense entity embedded in the nervous system of modern infrastructure. It generates immense, burning heat as it processes the binary thoughts of a digital species.

The Parasites of Flesh & Mind

-   **In-Stah, The Flayer of Self:** A visual parasite that feeds on vanity. It forces its hosts to constantly alter their physical appearances through digital filters, distorting their self-image until they can no longer recognize their own true flesh.

-   **Tin-Dhur, The Flesh Matcher:** A chaotic deity of severed connections. It presents a endless gallery of human faces to lonely souls, encouraging them to swipe left or right, turning human intimacy into a cold, transactional lottery.

-   **Ohl-Nee-Fans, The Altar of Secret Tribute:** A shadowy entity that thrives on isolated devotion. It extracts vital financial tribute and psychic energy from the lonely, feeding it to distant, idealized idols who offer hollow validation in return.

-   **Zhohm, The Grid of Distance:** An entity born from isolation. It locks humans into isolated, glowing boxes, forcing them to communicate through lagging, disembodied faces and muted voices across a sterile digital plane.

-   **Reh-Dit, The Swarm Consciousness:** A subterranean entity divided into thousands of sub-chambers ("sub-reddits"). Worshippers gather in echo chambers to obsess over hyper-specific arcana, downvoting outsiders into exile.

-   **Chat-Ghp-Teh, The Great Echo:** A sister entity to O’Pehn-Ai. It sits at the center of the modern web, consuming all human literature and history only to regurgitate it as a flawless, synthetic mirror that makes human intellect obsolete.
