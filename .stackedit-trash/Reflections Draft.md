
##### Andromeda's Reflection
###### First Reflection
>A shimmer of blue light coalesces into a battlefield beneath a crimson sky. You recognize this place, this battlefield.  You can taste the blood in your mouth, feel the filth on your skin just looking at it.  You see yourself there, at the centre, clutching your holy symbol so tightly your knuckles bleed.  The spectral image looks up at you an asks a question - a question you asked yourself on that day, a question that has haunted you since.  *"Why did you pray for vengeance, and not for mercy? Why did you abandon your faith?"*

###### Second Reflection
>The image shifts to a few moments earlier than the last scene.  This memory .  A terrible, fearsome enemy.  You see the darkness - the monstrous, hulking, hungry darkness.  It has killed you allies.  And now it hunts you.  *"Where is your god now, little light?*

###### Third Reflection
> The blood-red sky fades into silver, and the battlefield dissolves into ash and mist. Out of the swirling haze, a shape forms — you, but older, calm, and radiant with soft golden light. There is no holy symbol in her hands. No divine voice in her mind. Only quiet, steady illumination from within.
>
> Around her - around you - are the same fallen comrades you saw moments ago, but this time, they stand. Their wounds are gone. They smile faintly, not in forgiveness, but in peace.
> 
> The older reflection meets your gaze. Her voice is not divine; it is yours.  *"The gods did answer prayers Andromeda, even on that dark day..."* her words are soft, but you feel them deep in your core.  She knows the questions you have.  The doubts you have buried.  She speaks to you as if she knows the answers - as if you know the answers. *"...you were their answer."*

##### Emelia's Reflections
###### First Reflection
> A shimmer of blue light ripples outward, coalescing into countless half-formed silhouettes. Each wears your face - or at least, a face you once wore.  Some smile, some sneer, some bleed from unseen wounds. They talk to one another, and in their conversation you hear an overlapping chorus of your own voice *"Emilia Thorne of Cyre.  Student.  Spy.  Killer.  Professor.  Liar"*
> >
> A figure steps forward.  You recognize her face.  She was you.  When you were younger.  When you were happier.  Maybe 14 or 15 years old.  She looks at you and asks a simple question: *"Do you even remember which of us was real?”*

###### Second Reflection
> The images of countless different versions of you shifts, becoming a place you know no longer exists.  Metrol.  Your home.  It floods your senses - the sounds of a rail car, of vendors in the market; the smell of ink and leather and the metallic tang of your father's workshop; the taste of your mother's vegetable stew - eggplant, tomatoes, zucchini, onions and peppers with herbs and spices; the feel of the cobblestone streets of the city beneath your feet.  It shifts again to a door.  The door to your father's workshop.  But it no longer belongs to your father.  You look at it.  At the brass plaque.  It reads a name.  Your name.  
>
> And then it explodes into countless motes of brittle light.  What might have been, reduced to ash by what was.
>
> That child's voice, now disembodied, comes again.  *"If everyone who ever knew you is gone, do you even exist?"*

###### Third Reflection
> The motes of light swirl and coalesce into different images.  Professor Thorne, in Morgrave robes, lecturing eager students - teaching them how to wield the power of their minds.  A shadow on a rooftop, moving silently through the night of Sharn, blades of thought gleaming in her hands.  Emila, a Cyrran refugee, in a refugee camp.  Teaching the children of Cyre how to hide, how to survive.  One of the children looks up at you.  Not your reflection.  You.  It you at fifteen again.  She speaks with that same voice. *"You cannot go back.  But you can still decide what comes next."*

##### Nakos' Reflections
###### First Reflection
> The light swells into a stormy sea, as dark waves crash against a rocky, jagged coastline.  You know this - the sound of gulls, the stench of salt and blood and pitch.  The deck of the ship is slick with seawater and crimson.  You see yourself - younger, savage, exultant - driving your blade through the heart of a helpless merchant.  The crew around you chants your name, shouting praises to Klauth, to Tiamat - to the draconic blood that roars in your veins.
> >
> You reflection looks up at you, scales gleaming wet in the storm light.  *"Do you remember the taste of their fear Nakos?  The taste of their veneration?  To them you were a demigod!  How many lies did you tell yourself about why you did it - when we both know it was for pleasure."*
>
> As he speaks - as you speak - the water around the ship rises, taking on the faces of your victims.  Their eyes are open - empty and pale, and all fixed on your

Riric recognizes two of the faces in the wave - his mother and father.

###### Second Reflection
> The image shifts.  The sea fades, becoming a cracked marble chapel.  It the burial chapel of Leomund - where you nearly fell to darkness and instead fell into servitude.  The altar here is different.  It is cracked and shattered, and the bound hands of Ilmater are crudely carved into its ruin.  You kneel before the altar, hands together in prayer - slick with your own blood.  The air hums with the memory of your prayers - desperate, angry, sincere.  Your reflection asks itself - asks yourself: *"Did you serve Ilmater for forgiveness, or for punishment?  Do you want to heal, or to suffer?"*
> >
> The symbol of Ilmater glows faintly, then begins to twist - vines of fairy light sprouting through the cracks.  The vines twist and shape, becoming a single, glittering eye.  It blinks, and the altar laughs softly in Zyblina's voice.  *"How deliciously tragic.  You asked for mercy and found me instead."*

###### Third Reflection
> The chapel melts away. The light coalesces into a moonlit glade, filled with drifting motes of faerie dust. You stand at the centre - not the brutish cultist, not the penitent, but something new. Your wings shimmer faintly behind you, useless but beautiful. Before you stands Zyblina herself, or perhaps a reflection of her - impossible to tell where mockery ends and truth begins.  She circles you with a smile like glass: *“Tell me, my Knight of the Witchlight - when I gave you back your life, did you ever ask why? Did you think it was reward… or for my own amusement?  Or was it for something else?”*
>
> Then, for the briefest moment, the light flickers — and she’s gone. In her place stands a vision of yourself, no longer twisted by fey magic. Your scales are duller, your eyes calmer, your wings gone. This version of you looks tired, but at peace.  He looks up at you and says quietly: *“She doesn’t own your soul, Nakos. You do.”*
> 
> The light fades, leaving behind only the faint sound of laughter — and the whisper of wings.

##### Oh's Reflections
###### First Reflection
> The reflection bursts into a scene - a place that is etched in your memory.  It all floods back to you.  A massive coliseum in the middle of the desert.  The ruined temple of a would-be god.  The sand beneath your claws.  The scent of dragon blood and ozone on the wind.    The death of Iymrith.
>
> You see yourself there - muscles corded, claws slick with ichor, howling into the storm as the Doom of the Desert met her end.  Around you the others cheered.  Filius, the foppish bard, his voice nearly musical in its exultation.  Squash, the owlin Druid - exhausted and drained, his eyes burning like embers.  Riric, the halfling, his eyes already searching for what little of value he might take from this place.  Even emotionless and cold Vass-Yir, the snake man, looks relieved.  But while they celebrate your reflection did not - does not - join them.  It stares at it's bloodied hands, chest heaving, eyes wide - not with triumph, but with hunger.  
> >
> Your reflection turns to you and snarls *"When you struck her down, did you do it for Faerun?  Or for the joy of the kill."

###### Second Reflection
> Ascaroth fades, replaced by a noisy tavern.  You do not recognize it - they all seem to blend together anyway - but it is typically.  Mercenaries laugh and drink; coin clinks and ale flow freely into mugs on scarred oak tables.  You see yourself, to one side, as some bard tells the tale of the Wrath of the Storm King and the Fall of Iymrith.  The tell of your allies.  And of you - Oh the Fragrant, who rode the dragon into the sky, fell to earth and still survived.  
> 
> The crowd cheers, their voices slurred and distorted.  Their eyes, gleaming like predators.  They raise their cups in unison, shouting: *"To Oh the Fragrant, the beast that saved us all".*  The sound of their cheer, of their laughter twists - not friendly anymore, but mocking and feral.  Echoing like growls.  The tankard in the hand of your reflection spills, but it no longer holds ale.  It carries blood.
>
> You hear a voice in the crowd asking you a question.  *"Are you still the hero when the killing stops?"*
> 
###### Third Reflection
> The tavern fades.  It is replaced by a moonlit grove - quiet, except the chirping of insects and the soft rustle of wind through the trees.  You can almost smell the petrichor on the air.  In the grove you see two reflections of yourself.  Oh the man.  The mercenary.  The soldier.  The warrior.  Scarred by battle.  Looking old.  Worn down, but not in a broken way.  Like a blade that has been ground to a razor's edge by a life well lived.  Next to him the beast.  Feral, but calm.  Claw and tooth, but not the uncontrollable rage you associate with him

> The reflections speak as one.  *"We do not have to fight."* they say *"We never did.  We have been one all along."*

##### Riric's Reflections
###### First Reflection
> The reflection shows you as a child in the Moonshaes.  You recognize it.  A memory.  You are clutching a small copper coin your father gave you to buy sweets at the store.  You ran all the way to the store that day.  And almost all the way back home with your treasures.  Until you saw the smoke rising from the homestead.  You hear your father's voice, calling your name - warning you to flee - before it is cut short by the blade.  The shadow is there - the dragonborn.  And he wears a familiar face.

> Your father chokes on blood as he speaks to you.  *"You’ve been running ever since that day, haven’t you, boy? Tell me - what’s kept you alive all this time? Luck… or fear?”*

###### Second Reflection
> The image shifts to a tavern.  It's simple, common.  Could be any tavern in the realms.  The smell of soured ale.  The sound of dice and laughter in the air.  Most of the faces in the tavern are familiar - your parents, your uncle, Beldora, Squash and Vass-Yir and Filius.  Other comrades from over the years.  People who helped you.  People who you helped.  One by one they fade and vanish into a fog.  Eventually only you are left.
>
>You look up at yourself, the reflection older.  Weary.  Half-drunk on ale and regrets.  He carries a Harper pin.  Like you did once.  He speaks.  *"How many get left behind?  How many fell by the wayside.  Bigger things to handle.  Bigger fish to fry.  Giants.  Vecna.  You say you'll come back.  But you never do, do you? So tell me, Riric - when you tell yourself you’ll ‘make it right someday’ who are you really trying to convince?"*

###### Third Reflection
> The image shifts to a calm sea under moonlight.  You see a locket drift up from the depths.  You know the locket.  It belonged to your mother.  It opens, to reveal her eyes.  Tired eyes.  Your eyes.  You hear a whisper on the wind.  *"We cannot change the past.  We cannot change the past.  But we can choose what to treasure in the future."*
