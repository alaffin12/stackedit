> As you round a corner into what was once a bustling marketplace you are greeted by a scene of utter chaos and carnage. The once-bustling square is now overrun by a sea of undead. Broken stalls and toppled crates litter the ground, while the half-eaten remains of civilians and defenders alike like strewn across the cobblestones. A sickly, green fog hangs low in the area, the stench of death and decay almost unbearable.
>
>And in the middle of it you see an odd figure. A kobold, wearing a machine like a suit of armor, wielding a jet black sword almost as long as he is tall, cackling nearly madly as he throws himself into the horde.  He sees you as he runs an undead horror through with his sword.  "Hey you.  I know you.  Mistress send me to find you!  Xheklik come to help save the world again.  I guess.  Got anything to eat?"
