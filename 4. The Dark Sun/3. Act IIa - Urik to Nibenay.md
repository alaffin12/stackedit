# Into the Desert
Because he has a vested interest in the success of the players (as they are to retrieve for him the Lens of Rajaat, a powerful relic from a bygone era which will make Hamanu the most powerful Sorcerer-King save perhaps Borys himself) is sending a caravan with the players to ensure their survival in the harsh wastes of Athas.  This will be broken into two parts - the trip from Urik to Nibenay, which will be a slower moving caravan featuring a full complement of slaves; and the trip from Nibenay to the Pristine Tower, which will see the slaves and most of the caravan left behind in favour of fast moving crodlus and a company of Urikite Templars for protection.

Players will have to manage food and water supplies, as well as the morale of their travelling companions.  Running out of food and water in the desert is deadly, but keeping the spirits of their companions high is also important - low morale can result in calamity out on the dunes.  Templar morale starts at 5, while slave morale starts at 4.  The caravans will also encounter some of the harsh realities of life on Athas.

For every day on the road players will have to make various choices and rolls to see how they survive the day itself.

## Daily Checks
Every day the party will have to make checks to survive on the road.  One check is made during the midday short rest (no one with any sense travels during the warmest part of the day in the desert) and one is made at night during the long rest.  

**Midday Check:** DC 20 Consitution saving throw.  Players wearing medium or heavy armor have disadvantage on this save.  Players who have resistance to fire damage make this save automatically.  


### Environment - Hot & Cold
 The environment of Athas is a dire one, with scorching hot days alternating with bitterly cold nights.  

**While in the cities:** Players will have to make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw once a day to maintain their fitness.  Players who spend most of their day in medium or heavy armour have disadvantage on this save.  Players who have resistance to fire or cold damage, or whose background would suggest a reason to be adapted to the desert life can make this save with advantage.  On a failure the player gets one level of exhaustion.

**While travelling:** Travelling is harder.  During the day players must make a DC 20 Constitution save against heat stroke.  Players who spend their time travelling in medium or heavy armour, or who did not prepare adequately to travel in the desert, take disadvantage on this save.  Players who are resistant to fire damage automatically make this save.  On a failure the player takes a level of exhaustion.  During the long rest players must make a DC 15 Constitution save against the cold of the desert night.  Players who did not prepare adequately to travel in the desert take disadvantage on this save.  Players who are resistant to cold damage automatically make this save.  On a failure the player takes a level of exhaustion.
