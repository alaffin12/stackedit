### Preparations
Here the caravan slims down as it prepares to make a push through the Beast Barrens to the Tower.  The Alluvial Wastes are harsher than the regular desert, and consequently things will be difficult for the next seven days.  Sereth will trade the entire caravan and the slaves for 18 crodlu and 252 units of food/water (which will be enough for seven days travel at 2 units/day for each of them - the maximum amount of food/water a crodlu can carry).  For every slave that died or otherwise left camp on the journey to Nibenay remove 5 units of food/water from the provisions.  For every Templar that died or that they leave behind (or that left camp) add 10 units of food/water, but remember that this also reduces the capacity of the entire group - i.e. with one Templar missing the entire group can only carry 238 units of food/water.  Extra crodlu can be purchased by sacrificing 5 units of food/water from the total.
