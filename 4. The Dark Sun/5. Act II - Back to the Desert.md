### Preparations
Here the caravan slims down as it prepares to make a push through the Alluvial Wastes to the Tower.  The Alluvial Wastes are harsher than the regular desert, and consequently things will be difficult for the next seven days.  Sereth will trade the entire caravan and the slaves for 18 crodlu and 252 units of food/water (which will be enough for seven days travel at 2 units/day for each of them - the maximum amount of food/water a crodlu can carry).  For every slave that died or otherwise left camp on the journey to Nibenayremove 5 units of food/water from the provisions.  For every Templar that died or that they leave behind add 10 units of food/water.
