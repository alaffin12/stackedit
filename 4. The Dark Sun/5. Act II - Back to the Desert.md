### Preparations
Here the caravan slims down as it prepares to make a push through the Alluvial Wastes to the Tower.  The Alluvial Wastes are harsher than the regular desert, and consequently things will be difficult for the next seven days.  Sereth will trade the entire caravan and the slaves for 18 crodlu and 7 days of provisions
