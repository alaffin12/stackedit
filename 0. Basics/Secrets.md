
#### The Secret of Eberron
The Day of Mourning was caused by an attempt by Cyre to create a new god.  The Lord of Blades was to be this new god, but the ritual failed due to a single warforged refusing.  The backlash from the failed ritual destroyed Cyre and upended all of Eberron

#### The Secret of Ravenloft
Ravenloft exists as a place to trap the worst of the worst within their own stories, so that they are never able to escape.  Every story is the same - an offer of power leads to a betrayal, which chains the prisoner to the narrative prison. 

### The Secret of Athas
The Rhulisti of Athas tried to wield the Ritual of Remaking to reorder their own world.  They destroyed their own core truth, causing reality to begin to unravel.  The Pristine Tower was created to serve as an artificial core truth, but the price of the artificial truth has been dire.
