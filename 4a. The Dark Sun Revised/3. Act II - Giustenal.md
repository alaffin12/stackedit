# Giustenal
The road to New Giustenal is a rough one, but the party should not have much trouble making it with the assistance of Sethara and their kank mounts

#### Sethara
Sethera is the appointed companion of the party, their guide to the sands of Athas.  She is young, but able and experienced.  Her orders are help the strangers so while she may occasionally treat them in a patronizing manner if they do not know how to survive the desert, she is generally somewhat approaching friendly.  If Andromeda has become a Templar Wife she will offer her more respect, but possibly less friendliness - she views Andromeda as a tool in the hands of the Shadow King, just as she is - but a tool she is unfamiliar with

##### History
Sethara was born to no name worth remembering and learned early that survival in Nibenay belonged to the quick and the ruthless. Orphaned young, she grew up in the city’s back alleys and markets, running with a small gang of street children who stole ceramic bits and food to live another day; even then, it was Sethara who led them, deciding when to run, when to fight, and when to vanish. When a younger child under her protection was caught stealing and sentenced to lose his hands, Sethara attacked the guards and failed—then, bloody and desperate, she offered her own hands in his place. As the merchant prepared to take them, Nibenay himself intervened, seeing in her defiance and loyalty a blade worth forging rather than breaking. She was taken into the city’s forces, trained hard and without mercy, and at sixteen was elevated as a Templar Wife of the Chamber of Fire. Now, at twenty-six, Sethara is considered a senior wife within the chamber—respected, feared, and marked by the scar on her left hand, a reminder of the choice that cost her childhood and earned her a place beside a Sorcerer-King.

#### Kanks
Kanks are giant, horse sized ants that are common to the desert.  They have saddles and stirrups, but they will be uncomfortable for the party to ride, and Sethara may snicker a little when they struggle to get on the things.  

## Journey to the City
The journey is going to be largely ornamental, but every day will feature an opportunity for the party to interact with Sethara and learn about Athas and some kind of event.  Both will set the mood and give the party a better feel for Athas.

#### Sethara
Sethara is somewhat brusque when it comes to the party.  She is a professional soldier in the employ of her husband, the Shadow King and that will always shine through.  However she is curious about these strangers (although she has no concept of where they are from - she knows they are from far away, but as an Athasian she has no concept of the planes) and will ask them questions about their homes and history, contrasting it to her own.  Additionally if Andromeda became a Templar Wife she will try and learn about her specifically - they are sisters-in-arms now.  Alternatively, if Andromeda turned down the Shadow King she will be a bit more cool and standoffish towards her, wondering how anyone could turn down such power.  There are a couple of standard responses she will have:

* *If the party reveal metallic weapons/armor* "In your lands... is this common?  Here a thing like that can buy a city's loyalty - or its hatred.  But you carry it as if it were merely a tool."
* *If the party reveals their immense wealth (by Athasian standards)* "My huband told me you were from a land of plenty, but I think he was understating it.  You would need an army to protect so much.  Do you know how many eyes that would buy in Cromlin?"  She shakes her head "Here wealth is not kept.  It is spent - to make allies appear, threats vanish or to keep death away for another day.  Choose which you want before you show that much again."
* *If the party uses divine (elemental) magic* "Here, that kind of power attracts expectations.  Some will see protectors.  Others property.  Still others adversaries.  None will see travellers from a fantastic land."  She shakes her head "Use it when you must.  Hide it when you can.  Not because it is wrong, but because this land does not forgive being reminded that it can be commanded."
* *If the party uses preserving (arcane) magic* "You exhibit control.  Good.  But be warned.  Most here do not know the difference between what you did and what you could have done.  All are taught that such power is a wound waiting to be opened.  Others will see the gesture and feel the fear they have been taught.  If others ask, let me speak.  If others accuse, let me stand between you and them.  I see.  I know.  I have been told.  But Athas has not."
* *If the party uses defiling (arcane) magic* "You take.  You take and take and take and leave nothing but ash." She shakes her head "My husband wields such power.  As do others in his employ.  I will not pretend I have never seen one such as you.  One who defiles.  But it sets me on edge nonetheless.  Understand - I will not stop you.  I will not betray you.  My king - my husband - has commanded me to walk with you.  To accompany you.  I am loyal to him.  Loyal to you.  But right now I would like to see you split open for doing what you just did.  If you must kill this land - if you, from your land of plenty and excess, must steal what little Athas has - then do so.  But do so where no one is left to remember."

### Roc Attack
> As afternoon passes into evening Sethara begins to look up at random times.  She's obviously watching for something.  And then she sees it.  "Dismount...'" she says quietly, quickly "...we are being stalked and the kanks are not trained for battle.  We will lead the hunters away on foot, deal with them and return.  The hunters will follow us, not the kanks - they are very good at hiding in the desert."  She points upwards "Roc.  I count four.  Stay low.  Be careful.  If they carry you upwards it is a long way down."

This can be done theatre of the mind, or it can be done on a blank battle map, since the terrain is generally featureless.  The roc are vicious hunters, and know how to attack.  Starting at about 100 ft they dive bomb the smallest, most exposed target.  In one turn they drop and attack with their claws.  If they hit they carry their prey 20 ft in the air and attack it a second time with their beak.  The next round they move back up to 100 ft and proceed to attack their captured prey with their beak until it dies/breaks free at which point they simply line up for a new bombing run.  If they miss they will attack with their beak and retreat to 20 ft, taking attacks of opportunity to fly away, doing the same attack from 20 ft next round (retreating to 100 ft after the attack).  

Remember falling damage is 1d6 per 10 ft of falling to a maximum of 20d6 (so if players drop from 20 ft they will take 2d6 falling damage; from 100 ft they will take 10d6 falling damage).
