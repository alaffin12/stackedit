Presumably the party leave Giustenal and begins to make preparations to head to the Pristine Tower.  Sethara has this to say about the Pristine Tower.  

> **"I do not know which is worse.  That I know Dregoth is heading to the Pristine Tower.  Or that I myself am oath-bound to follow him.  Skies - this is worst news we could have heard.  I am a Templar Wife of Nibenay.  I will go.  But know this - I do not expect to come back.  And if I do I do not know what I will be when I return."**

The Pristine Tower is known to her, at least vaguely - to the south, close to the shores of the Silt Sea, but upon the other side of the head of land (think like Bay Bulls to Holyrood, but wider and with silt instead of water and desert instead of boreal forest).  **"It is not an easy journey.  There is an oasis we will head for first.  From there we will have to skirt the Great Ivory Plain until we reach the Crossing Mountain - the only safe place to cross the plain in this region that I know.  Then... who knows.  The Pristine Tower is a dangerous secret strangers.  I know of it.  I know what it means.  But I have never been.  I will pray to my husband tonight to take this task from me.  To come and walk with you himself.  He will not answer me.  I know that.  But I will pray nonetheless.  And I will pray that he shows us the way beyond the crossing.  Because I do not know it."**

As the party sits around a campfire that evening...

> As you all sit and contemplate the journey before you there is a sharp pain in all of your heads.  You close your eyes for a moment, focusing away the pain, and when you open them again you are greeted by seven spectral figures sitting around the fire with you.  Each of them is tall, and although they are mere projections they cast long, draconic shadows in the firelight,  
> 
>One of them you recognize - the Sorcerer-King Nibenay.  The other six are similar in stature if not dress - a dark-skinned young woman with long, silky black hair and a green cloak; a gaunt old man with crimson robes, feathered ornaments, and a skull-like face hidden beneath elaborate golden paint; a towering bronze-skinned warrior with leonine features, golden eyes, and a mane of dark hair streaked with gray; a regal woman with copper skin, sharp features, jeweled silks, and eyes like burning amber; a tall, pale man in flowing white robes whose faintly luminous skin and calm eyes seem almost inhuman; and an immense woman with obsidian skin, a shaved head adorned in gold rings, and heavy robes trimmed with crimson scales.
>
> You watch as Sethara drops - almost collapses - to her knees in pure prostration, her body tense with fear and dread and awe.  The old man in crimson and feathers speaks first, staring at all of you with deep piercing eyes.  **"It is customary to kneel in the presence of divinity, is it not?"**  His voice resonates.  It demands compliance.  But then another voice cuts through it, the tension not snapping, but fading.
>
> **"I told you they are not of Athas Tectuktitlay.  They are from a strange world beyond Athas.  Beyond the grey and black.  They do not know much about us.  Only what little I have told them and perhaps what they have learned from walking in this land."**  He turns to you all and smiles **"But you are correct - it is customary to kneel in the presence of divinity.  I present to you Lalali-Puy, Tectuktitlay, Manu, Uyness, Keltis and Kalid-Ma."**
>
> **"Gallard..."** Lalali-Puy snaps **"...at least do us the honour of using our proper names.  Not the ones we shed an age ago."**
>
> Nibenay - or is that Gallard - sighs and shakes his head.  **"Very well.  Sethara, my bride, would you introduce our esteemed guests?"**
>
> Sethara is still on her knees, still prostrated before the seven.  **"Y-yes my husband-king..."** she says, her voice shaky and uncertain **"...they are the Oba, Goddess-Queen of Gulg who commands the lethal fury of the Crescent Forest; Tectuktitlay, the Mighty King of Draj, Master of the Black Pyramid, who feeds his soil with the blood of thousands; Hamanu, the Lion of Urik, the Master of War, whose armies never break; Ablach-Re, the Grand Queen of Raam, the Voice of the Skies; Oronis, the Wise Monarch of Kurn; and Kalid-Ma, the Reborn Scourage of Kalidnay, who has shattered the bonds of death itself.  Once they wore the names my Dread Husband spoke.  They were the Champions of Raajat.  Now they are the Masters of Athas."**
>
>**"Not all..."** Nibenay corrects **"...but all those I could convince to convene here.  We have come to help you.  To guide you.  To inform you."**

> **"Nibenay tells us that Dregoth has gone to the Pristine Tower."** Oronis says **"That he has the help of an outsider who seeks to upset Athas.  Dregoth alone is a concern.  An age ago we came together - the last time we came together - to cast him down.  Now Dregoth is with an ally such as this outsider is a problem that must be met head on.  Unfortunately we are short on time and options."**
>
> Hamanu picks up where Oronis left off.  **"When we last moved against him we marched on Giustenal, with Ablach-Re and I in the van.  But we have been caught unawares.  We do not have time to marshal our strength.  And he has taken the Pristine Tower.    A place we cannot go ourselves."**
>
> Kalid-Ma picks up: **"Not even for all our power.  Understand - the Pristine Tower is a place of change.  Rajaat forged us one and all there.  But Rajaat could control the power of the tower.  We cannot.  Perhaps Dregoth has discovered how.  Perhaps the outsider whispered its secrets to him.  We do not know.  But if we go there we might begin to change.  And that change..."**
>
> Nibenay shakes his head.  **"The world has one dragon.  Borys.  It cannot sustain another."**
>
> Ablach-Re speaks next after a moment's silence.  **"Tonight you sleep under the protection of the Sorcerer-Kings.  Tonight you may rest.  Tonight you may speak to us as equals and ask of us what you will.  Tomorrow you will awake near the Bogo Forest.  As close as we can get you to the Pristine Tower.  You will go forward from there with our blessings, in the hopes that you can prevent this calamity."** 

The Sorcerer-Kings are the most knowledgeable people in all of Athas when it comes to the Pristine Tower, but even they have limits.
