# Outline
This battle will consist of four phases (although perhaps it would be better to describe it as a role play phase and three active combat phases).  

## Phase I: The Final Climb
> The final stair winds upward in silence.
>
> Gone is the low hum of the reality machinery below.  Gone is the weight of ancient purpose etched into the flawless stone of the tower.  Even your footsteps seem muffled, as though your destination is swallowing sound itself.  An odd radiance spills down the stairwell.  Not sunlight, nor firelight - not even light truly.  It is more the absence of darkness - frail, fragile and uncertain, but cleaner.

Gone are the echoes of the tower below. Gone is the weight of ancient stone. Even your footsteps seem muffled, as though the chamber ahead swallows sound itself.

A pale radiance spills down the stairwell—not sunlight, nor firelight, but something older. Cleaner. It is the color of possibility.

The stair opens into an immense circular chamber, nearly one hundred and thirty-five feet across. Its domed ceiling vanishes into brilliance high overhead, where threads of living light drift like slow-moving constellations.

The floor is fashioned from flawless white crystal veined with shimmering gold, every line part of an impossibly intricate arcane design.

At the chamber's heart rises a broad dais of polished stone. Resting within it is an enormous mechanism unlike anything any of you have ever seen. Rings of crystal, adamant, and impossibly smooth white metal rotate soundlessly around one another. Gears turn without touching. Runes ignite and extinguish in complex patterns. Every movement is impossibly precise, as though the machine itself were thinking.

Around the mechanism stretches an immense circle of luminous sigils nearly eighty feet across. Five lesser circles are woven into its outer edge, each glowing with its own impossible hue.
