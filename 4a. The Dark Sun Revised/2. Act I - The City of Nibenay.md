# Nibenay
## Arrival
> You do not so much arrive in the city as it collapses into place around you.  You step through the boundary between world, through endless void, and into a narrow back alley - hemmed in by narrow stone walls that lean inwards above you.  Beneath your feet the ground is not soil, nor stone, but dust - powdered and compacted and shaped into a foot-path, yet ground so fine that each step coats your boots with grey.
