# Nibenay
## Arrival
> You do not so much arrive in the city as it collapses into place around you.  You step through the boundary between world, through endless void, and into a narrow back alley - hemmed in by narrow stone walls that lean inwards above you.  Beneath your feet the ground is not soil, nor stone, but dust - powdered and compacted and shaped into a foot-path, yet ground so fine that each step coats your boots with grey and hangs gently in the air, like a cloud.
>
> The air!  It is hot, but it is not the clean heat of summer.  It is not even the humid warmth of the jungle.  The heat is baked it.  It is oppressive, laying upon you like a weight.  It feels as if the city itself has been cooking, trapped in an over for centuries and never cooled.  Every breath feels dry, rasping and faintly acrid.
> 
> The is no birdsong here.  No insects buzzing.  No distant wind rustling through the leaves.  There is a constant low murmur.  The scrape of sandals on dirt, the clink of bone and ceramic and the hiss of whispered bargains and secrets.  The city - like all cities - is alive.  But it does not sing.  It grinds.  
>
> You look up at the narrow rays of the sun, slicing into the alleyway.  You see walls of dark, smooth stone, carved with intricate, almost hypnotic patterns that serpentine back and forth around itself.  The light shimmers near the wall, and you know the stones absorb heat.  You would burn yourself, if you could touch them.
>
> A pair of locals pass by the mouth of the alley.  They do not stop.  They do not stare.  The avoid all eye contact and keep their heads down, robes pulled tight against the sun.  But you know they have seen you.  Marked your arrival, though they know not what it means.
>
> You step out of the alley, into the streets of the city, and move around for a bit.  The people here do not speak common, and you do not speak whatever language they do.  It isn't long before someone comes up to you, speaking quickly in the strange language of the locals, making gestures as if urgently trying to get you to move.  Eventually she sighs softly, as if she is confirming something she already suspected.  
>
>*"You... not from here."*  It's not so much a question as it is a declaration.  *"You look wrong.  Walk wrong.  This city, it eat people who walk like you."*  She shakes her head *"This alley.  Not allowed.  Wives only.  You understand?  Big trouble.  Wives come and take you."*  She looks at you carefully, sizing you up, evaluating you.  Her common might be broken and halting, but the look your recognize.  It is the same look a merchant gives when you ask to buy from their special stock, their private collection.  She's trying to decide if you are worth her time.  Eventually she decides you are.  *"Tzani help you?  Tzani knows streets.  Knows rules.  Know who hurt you if you break rules.  You want water?  Want place to sleep where knife not come in night?  Tzani show."*  She pauses *"Not free.  Tzani need bits too.  Need food, need water too.  First lesson free.  Do not go down alley.  Second lesson need pay."*
